# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Bukmark serves self-hosters and power users managing large bookmark collections on trusted LAN or Tailscale environments. They capture quickly, return later, and need to search, filter, group, and triage without turning capture into an organizing chore.

## Product Purpose

Bukmark makes unsorted bookmarks a valid working state. It captures pages immediately, keeps large collections fast to browse, and supports deliberate triage through search, hubs, bulk actions, exports, and Claude-assisted sorting. Success means users can save without hesitation and later recover, understand, and organize what matters.

## Brand Personality

Warm, blunt, capable. The product communicates directly, feels intentional under heavy use, and avoids decoration that competes with the work.

## Anti-references

- SaaS metric dashboards that promote aggregate numbers over the current task.
- Decorative micro-labels, badges, stamps, and ornamental metadata.
- Rounded, shadowed component kits that soften every control into the same generic shape.
- Browser-default controls that make an otherwise designed interface feel unfinished.
- Conditional interface bars or panels that unexpectedly move the user's content.

## Design Principles

1. Treat unsorted as a useful state, not an error to hide.
2. Keep triage predictable. Selection, filtering, and bulk actions must not move or obscure the user's place.
3. Put information where it changes a decision. Remove totals and metadata that do not help the current task.
4. Support dense collections without making controls cramped, ambiguous, or fragile.
5. Make self-hosting and data control explicit, trustworthy, and free of invented claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interactive controls must be fully keyboard operable, expose clear accessible names and states, retain visible focus, and never depend on color alone. Text and controls require readable contrast. Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences. Mobile layouts must avoid horizontal page scrolling and preserve usable touch targets and one-line action labels.
